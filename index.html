Rebooking and Cancellation Policy

A. Rebooking

  • Requests for rebooking should preferably be made at least seven (7) days before the reserved date.
  • All rebooking requests are subject to the availability of dates.
  • Rebooking is free of charge if requested at least seven (7) days before the reserved date.
  • Requests made five (5) or six (6) days before the reserved date shall be subject to a rebooking fee of ₱1,000.00, payable in full before the new date is confirmed.
  • Rebooking is no longer allowed if requested four (4) days or less before the reserved date.
  • A confirmed booking may be rebooked only once.

B. Cancellation

  • Cancellations made at least eight (8) days before the reserved date are entitled to a full refund of all payments made.
  • For cancellations made seven (7) or six (6) days before the reserved date, all payments shall be forfeited, and the reservation shall be considered consumed due to a “no-show.”
  • However, if the cancellation is due to a duly established force majeure or fortuitous event, the guest may choose either:
    • A refund equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of the total amount paid; or
    • A fifty percent (50%) discount on the guest’s next booking, subject to calendar availability.
  • If the cancellation is made five (5) days or less before the reserved date, all payments shall be forfeited, regardless of the reason for cancellation. The options granted for force majeure or fortuitous events shall no longer apply.
  • Failure to appear on the reserved date without a timely and valid cancellation shall be treated as a “no-show,” and all payments shall be forfeited.

Pet Policy 🐾

We happily welcome up to two (2) small-breed, potty-trained pets per villa. To help us keep the space fresh and comfortable for everyone, a sanitary deposit of ₱1,000 per pet will be collected upon reservation.

For everyone's comfort, we kindly ask responsible pet owners to have their dogs or cats wear diapers while inside the villa.

Before check-out, our caretaker will do a quick inspection. If there are noticeable odors from pet urine or feces in any room or interior area, a ₱1,000 charge per pet will be deducted from the sanitary deposit.

Any unused portion of the sanitary deposit will be return via GCash or Bank transfer.

Thank you for helping us keep our villas clean and pet-friendly. We look forward to welcoming you and your furry companions! 🐶🐱
